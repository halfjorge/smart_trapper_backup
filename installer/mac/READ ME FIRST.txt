SMART TRAPPER - installing on this Mac
======================================

1. Double-click "Install Smart Trapper.command".

2. The FIRST time only, the Mac may say it "can't be opened because Apple cannot check it"
   (it is our own shop tool, not from the App Store):
     - click Done (NOT Move to Trash)
     - open System Settings > Privacy & Security
     - scroll down to the message about "Install Smart Trapper" and click Open Anyway
     - type your Mac password if asked, then click Open
   A Terminal window opens and shows the steps; a message says when it's finished.

3. If Creative Cloud asks whether to install "Smart Trapper UXP", click Install.

4. Quit and reopen Photoshop. The panel is under Plugins > Smart Trapper UXP > Smart Trapper.

The guide (Smart_Trapper_Guide.pdf) is copied to your Home folder > SmartTrapper.
Problems: take a screenshot and send it to Sara.
